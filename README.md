## About this template

A one-page, one-column resume for developers. Section titles are small caps over a thin rule, with education, experience and projects.

## What is Transform Resume

A free resume builder with templates and an AI assistant.
- Click any line on the page and type. The layout stays put.
- Your PDF looks exactly like the page you see.
- Chat with the AI to change your resume for you.
- Set colors, fonts and spacing until it looks like you.
- Free to start, no card required.

## How to make a resume with this template

1. Open [Transform Resume](https://www.transformresume.com).
2. Pick Aru in the template gallery.
3. Add your own details in the editor. Export the PDF.
